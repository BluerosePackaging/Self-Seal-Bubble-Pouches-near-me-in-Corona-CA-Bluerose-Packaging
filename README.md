## How to Select the Right Pouch for Your Products

Before ordering, consider how the pouch will be used and what the contents need during shipment.

**1. Measure the product carefully**

Check the item's length, width, and thickness. Allow enough room to insert and remove it without forcing the product into the pouch. Confirm usable internal dimensions rather than relying only on the overall pouch size.

**2. Consider the level of cushioning**

A bubble-lined pouch can help with surface protection and minor impacts, but it may not provide enough protection for heavy, sharp-edged, or highly fragile products. Those items may need additional cushioning and a properly sized corrugated box.

**3. Check the closure and packing process**

Choose a pouch that allows the adhesive flap to close securely without pressing against the product. If your team packs many orders each day, a consistent pouch size can help simplify handling and inventory.

**4. Match packaging to the shipping route**

Consider the distance, handling conditions, and whether packages will be stacked or transported with other goods. Use an outer carton when the product needs structural support or additional protection against crushing.

**5. Review your order volume**

Estimate how many pouches your team uses during a typical week or month. Sharing expected quantities and preferred sizes can help when requesting a quote for ongoing packaging needs.

## Practical Tips for Packing and Storage

A few simple steps can improve how bubble pouches perform during everyday use.

- Remove sharp objects or rough edges that could puncture the pouch before packing.
- Place the product inside without stretching or overfilling the pouch.
- Press the adhesive flap firmly along its closure area.
- Add void fill inside an outer carton if the packed item can move around.
- Store unused pouches in a clean, dry area away from excessive heat and direct sunlight.
- Keep heavier materials from compressing the bubble lining during storage.

Remember that bubble pouches provide cushioning, not the same rigid support as a box. For fragile products or shipments exposed to rough handling, combine the pouch with suitable outer packaging.

The built-in adhesive strip is designed to close the pouch without additional tape. An outer carton may still need tape to secure it for shipment.

### 3. Can bubble pouches protect fragile items?

They can help cushion products against minor impacts and surface scratches. Highly fragile items may need extra padding and a sturdy outer box.

### 4. Are self-seal bubble pouches suitable for electronics?

They can be suitable for certain electronic accessories and components. For static-sensitive electronics, choose packaging specifically designed for electrostatic protection.

### 5. How do I choose the correct pouch size?

Measure the product's length, width, and thickness, then compare those measurements with the pouch's usable internal dimensions. Allow enough room for easy insertion and secure closure.

### 7. Can bubble pouches be used for storage?

Yes. They can help separate items and reduce surface scratches during storage. Keep them in a clean, dry place and avoid prolonged compression of the bubble lining.

Not in every situation. Pouches provide cushioning, while corrugated boxes offer structural support. Products that are heavy, fragile, or vulnerable to crushing may require both.
